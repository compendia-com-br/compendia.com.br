The proof is in the book

Check it yourself

When the text covers the question, the answer points to the volume and the exact sentence. When it doesn't, it says so instead of filling the gap.

Before a work goes in, it passes four checks

  1. Public domain, author by author, by date of death. The rule is the one where this service is operated: Brazilian copyright law (Law 9,610/98, art. 41: 70 years). Terms are counted differently elsewhere.
  2. Translation rights, separate from the original. Without the translator's date confirmed, the volume does not go on sale: today 182 volumes are held back.
  3. Co-authorship, read off the work, not the catalog: the title page decides.
  4. Text cleaned of the digitization notice, so a library's own note never comes back as a Bibliotheca quotation.

Questions

An ordinary AI assistant already answers this for free. Why pay?

It does answer. The question is whether you check. The Bibliotheca cites the author, the volume and the exact sentence, or tells you it found nothing. You can open the book and check.

What is this actually for?

Citing an author exactly in a paper, article or lecture; comparing what two authors wrote on the same subject.

How do I install it?

In Claude, as a plugin (the desktop app, claude.ai or Claude Code). In ChatGPT, as a plugin from the official directory, in the browser or the app. The full guide is open before you buy.

Is there an API key, or a connector for other assistants?

Being built.

Is the collection complete?

It is not a complete library of any field: it is the work of the forty-eight listed authors. And it does not provide a published English edition: the text stays in the language of each volume in the corpus, and the translation of the sentence is made on the spot.

What does the Bibliotheca not do?

It doesn't interpret an author for you, it doesn't summarize their “essence”, and it doesn't invent a citation when the corpus doesn't cover the question.
