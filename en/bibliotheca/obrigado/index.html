Installation and use

Your key arrives by email as soon as the payment clears. It starts with cmp_ and it is your license: the key is what opens the corpus, wherever you use it.

Key not there yet? Pix and card clear immediately; bank slip and transfer take longer. If it still hasn't arrived after that, message us on WhatsApp at +55 34 93618-0015 or write to contato@compendia.com.br.

Install and activate

This works in the desktop app, claude.ai and Claude Code. Installing in one does not install in the others.

The screenshots and videos here show Claude in English. The Compendia sign-in screen is in Portuguese for now: the field is Chave de acesso (access key) and the button is Entrar (sign in).

  1. Download the plugin

    Download compendia-bibliotheca.zip. The file is small and holds no books.

    Download the plugin

  2. Install

    • Click your name in the bottom-left corner.
    • Settings → Plugins.
    • Add → the option to upload a plugin.
    • Drag the file in and click the upload button.

    The red notice is Claude's standard warning for any plugin uploaded as a file.

  3. Activate it with your key

    • On the plugin page, Connectors tab, click Connect next to compendia.
    • Don't change anything in the window: confirm, and confirm again on the next screen.
    • Paste the cmp_ key and click Entrar.

    The notice «Connected to compendia» appears.

  4. Check the permission

    In Settings → Connectors, set compendia to «Needs approval» or «Always allow». With the connector blocked, the corpus does not answer.

The plugin from ChatGPT's official directory. It works in the browser and in the app, and there is no file to download.

These screenshots were taken with ChatGPT in Portuguese. The steps below describe each button, so you can find it in English.

  1. Open the plugin page

    Sign in to ChatGPT and open the Compendia Bibliotheca page. Click Install plugin, at the top right.

    Open in ChatGPT

  2. Sign in with your key

    ChatGPT opens the Compendia sign-in screen. Paste your cmp_ key and click Sign in.

  3. Done

    A notice says the plugin is installed, and the button changes to one that opens a test chat.

    If a «Configure» button shows up, you can click it: it only tests the connection and lists the authors your key covers. There is nothing to adjust.

  4. Use it

    In a new chat, type @compendia at the start of the message and pick Compendia Bibliotheca from the list. Then call the author with a slash, for instance /dewey.

Ways to use it

Talk to an author
Start with a slash and the name, for instance /steiner, followed by your question. In ChatGPT, writing the name at the start of the sentence also works.
See who your key reaches
Ask to list authors.
Ask for the citation
Ask for the exact sentence. The answer gives the volume and the sentence, and the page when the volume preserves it.
Look for a theme in the corpus
Ask where a subject appears. The search runs through the volumes your key covers.
Put two authors face to face
After the first one, call the second and ask what their text says about what was said.
One author at a time, on purpose: that way two voices never get mixed. That is why switching authors takes a few extra seconds.

When the subscription expires

Access runs for a year. You get an email warning seven days before it expires, and after that there are three days of grace in which everything keeps working.

Once the grace period passes without renewal, access pauses. Nothing is deleted: on renewal the same key works again, with no reinstalling and no pasting anything again.

If something doesn't work

The author answers without their voice

Call them with the slash, /steiner, in the first message. The slash is what loads the persona.

I installed it and it doesn't show up

In Settings → Connectors, check that compendia is connected and enabled, and that the permission is not blocked.

It works in one place and not the other

That is expected: the app, claude.ai, Claude Code and ChatGPT are separate installations. Repeat it in the ones you use.

It takes a while to answer

On the first question to each author, it loads the whole persona. The ones after that are faster.

It says the corpus doesn't cover my question

That is the system working, not failing: when the text does not cover the question, it says so instead of filling the gap.

I lost my key

It is in the email you received. Reply to that email and we will send it again. Don't generate a new one yourself.

The key is your license. Don't share it: whoever has the code has your access.
