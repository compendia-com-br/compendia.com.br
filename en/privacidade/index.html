Privacy Policy

Version 3 · 16 September 2026 · applies to compendia.com.br and to the service at corpus.compendia.com.br

Compendia is a corpus of works by classic authors, in the public domain, which artificial intelligence assistants (Claude, ChatGPT and the like) consult in order to locate and quote passages. The service is operated by Thiago Luz, in Brazil.

There are two different situations here, and they handle data differently. Searching the corpus neither requires nor produces data that identifies you. Buying does: to issue your key, we need to know who bought it. The sections below say which is which.

What reaches the server

When you talk to an author, the assistant you are using sends the server what the search needs: the term or passage being looked for, the author and, where relevant, the volume and page requested. The rest of your conversation does not arrive, nor does your name, nor your account details on the AI platform.

As with any service on the internet, the server automatically records the originating IP address, the time, the route called and the identifier of the program that called it. Those records exist for operation and security.

The access key

Access works through a key that identifies the licence — which authors are unlocked — and not the person. The key is held by whichever AI platform you configured, and travels only between that platform and this server.

For how long

Technical access records are deleted automatically within 14 days. The term you searched for is not stored in any database and is not linked to you; it exists only in those technical records, for the same period.

Measurement of browsing

Since 11 September 2026 this site loads two advertising measurement tags, on every page. Before that date there was no measurement at all, and this section said so — the policy promised to change on the same day the fact changed. That is what is being done here.

  1. Google Ads tag — identifier AW-18444114151.
  2. Meta Pixel (Facebook and Instagram) — identifier 981095944998918.

Why they exist: Compendia has started advertising, and these are what make it possible to know that a visit came from an ad we paid for. Without them there is no way to separate someone who arrived through search from someone who arrived through an ad.

What they do: they set a cookie in your browser and tell Google and Meta that this page was opened. The data is handled by them, each under its own policy.

They load on every page, without asking first. That is the fact today, and it is written here rather than left to be discovered.

What still does not exist: there is no Google Analytics, no heat mapping, no session recording and no usage profile built by us. The purchase happens off this site, on the payment platform. Meta receives no data about the purchase — not whether it happened, not the amount. Google does, but only after the purchase and only by the route described in Measuring whether a purchase came from an ad — not through this tag.

How to refuse: blocking third-party cookies in your browser, or using any ad blocker, stops both from loading. Nothing on the site stops working: the Bibliotheca, the search and your key are indifferent to them. This does not affect the sending described in Measuring whether a purchase came from an ad: that happens on the server, after the purchase, and does not go through the browser.

What we do not do

Nothing is sold. There is no marketing outreach: whoever buys receives the key and the notices belonging to their own subscription, and nothing beyond that. One piece of information is shared, with a single recipient and a single purpose: Google receives the buyer's e-mail address in scrambled form, to measure whether the purchase came from an ad. The details are in Measuring whether a purchase came from an ad.

When you buy

Payment is processed by Greenn, which operates the checkout. Card and payment details do not pass through us — they go straight to them, and what Greenn does with them is set out in their own policy. Anyone who bought up to September 2026 went through Cakto, which operated the checkout before; those subscriptions continue to be served, and what Cakto does with that data is set out in their own policy.

Once a purchase is approved, the payment platform tells us your name, your e-mail and, where you provide them, your tax number and telephone. We keep that data to issue and maintain your access key, to know what it unlocks and until when. There is a second use, of the e-mail address only, described in the next section. Beyond those two, there is no other.

Measuring whether a purchase came from an ad

This measurement starts working after this version is published; no purchase made before it is sent. When a purchase is approved, the server adds a row to a Google spreadsheet containing: your e-mail address scrambled by a mathematical formula (SHA-256), the date and time of the purchase, the total amount, the currency and the order number.

Scrambling protects the address, but it does not make the data anonymous. The e-mail is never sent in the clear. But anyone who already has your e-mail address can compute the same code and recognise it — which is precisely how the measurement works. It therefore remains personal data, and is treated as such.

What it is for: Google Ads reads the spreadsheet, at a scheduled time, and compares the code with that of anyone who clicked one of our ads while signed in to a Google account. If they match, the purchase is attributed to that click. That is the only purpose. Anyone who was not signed in to a Google account when they clicked is not identified by this method.

The order number is in the spreadsheet only so that the same sale is not sent twice. Google Ads does not use it.

What is not sent: your name, tax number, telephone, the e-mail in the clear, and what was bought. Meta receives none of this.

Where it is processed: Google may process this data on servers outside Brazil.

Why we do this without asking for separate consent: the legal basis is legitimate interest (Brazilian Law 13,709/2018, art. 7, IX) — knowing whether money spent on advertising brought in the sale. That is why the data is the minimum necessary and why you can object, as below.

For how long: the row is deleted from the spreadsheet within 90 days — the period in which Google still connects a click to a purchase. After that it serves no purpose.

How to refuse: write to contato@compendia.com.br asking us not to use your e-mail in this measurement. The row is deleted from the spreadsheet and your e-mail stops being sent for future purchases; your key carries on working. This refusal is different from refusing cookies, above: blocking a cookie does not stop this sending.

How the key reaches you

By e-mail, delivered by Resend, who are the ones that carry the message — the sending address is acervo@compendia.com.br. When you give a telephone number at purchase, the key is also sent by WhatsApp, from our own server and not from a bulk messaging platform.

These are messages about your purchase: the key, and then the notices belonging to the subscription itself. They do not go onto a marketing list.

How long we keep the record

For as long as your licence exists, because the record is what makes the key work. You can ask for deletion at any time — and, if you do, the key stops working with it, since the record is what sustains it.

Your rights

Under the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (Law 13,709/2018, the LGPD), you can ask for confirmation, access, correction or deletion of data about you, through the contact below. We answer these requests whoever you are and wherever you are writing from.

If you only searched the corpus, we hold nothing that identifies you beyond the temporary technical records — the request is settled by their expiry, within 14 days. If you bought, we hold the record described above, and a deletion request is carried out; the key stops working with it.

Contact

Responsible: Thiago Luz. Contact channel: contato@compendia.com.br.

Changes

If this policy changes, the version and date at the top change with it. The version in force is always the one on this page — and on the Portuguese page, which is the same policy.

Questions before subscribing: contato@compendia.com.br — we answer within 24 to 48 business hours.
