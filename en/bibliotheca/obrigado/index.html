Installation and use

Your key arrives by e-mail as soon as the payment clears. It starts with cmp_ and it is your licence — the key is what opens the corpus, wherever you use it.

Key not there yet? Pix and card clear immediately; bank slip and transfer take longer, and the e-mail only goes out after the payment is confirmed. If it still hasn't arrived after that, write to contato@compendia.com.br — we answer within 24 to 48 business hours.

1 · Install and use it in Claude

6 min 59 s · spoken in Portuguese, with two chapters: install and connect, then talk to the authors. The on-screen steps are the ones written below.

This works in all three Claude surfaces — the desktop app, claude.ai in the browser, and Claude Code. The path is the same in all three. But installing in one does not install in the others: if you use more than one, repeat it there.

Install the plugin

Download the plugin compendia-bibliotheca.zip

The file is the trigger for the Bibliotheca — small, with no books inside it. It only answers after you connect your key.

  1. Click your name, in the bottom left corner.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. In the left-hand menu, choose Plugins.
  4. In the top right corner, click Add and then Upload.
  5. Click Browse files and pick the .zip you downloaded.
  6. Open → Upload. The plugin is now installed and enabled.

Connect your key

  1. Still in Settings, click Connectors.
  2. Install → Continue → wait for it to finish.
  3. Scroll the add custom connector screen to the bottom and click Add.
  4. Click the connector again and choose Connect.
  5. It asks for the key. Paste yours — the one starting with cmp_ — and click Sign in.
  6. The page reloads, and a notice appears in the bottom right saying the connector is connected.

Check that it worked

Go back to your name → Settings → Connectors. compendia should show as connected and enabled.

That is also where you set the permission. Use Ask before using or Always allow — with the other options the corpus does not answer.

Talk to the authors

  1. Open a new conversation.
  2. Type a slash and the author's name — /steiner, for instance — and ask your question.
  3. To see which authors your key reaches, ask it to list authors.

One author at a time, and that is deliberate. The Bibliotheca loads each author's reconstructed voice separately, so that two voices never get mixed into one answer. That is why switching authors takes a few seconds — it is the next one loading.

You can put two authors in conversation in the same thread: after the first one answers, call the second and ask what he makes of it. What you get back is each corpus read in turn, with the citation attached — not the authors themselves.

Creating a Project in Claude is optional — it keeps a separate thread per author. The connector on its own already delivers everything.

2 · Use it in ChatGPT

5 min 05 s · spoken in Portuguese, with two chapters: open it and activate the key, then talk to the authors. The on-screen steps are the ones written below.

Nothing to download here. No file, no upload — just the link and your key.

Open Compendia Bibliotheca in ChatGPT

This is the GPT to use — the official one, already configured. Keep the link: it is how you come back.

  1. Sign in to your ChatGPT account.
  2. Open the GPT with the button above (or with the link you got by e-mail — it is the same one).
  3. Click list available authors or learn more about Compendia.
  4. A button appears to start the session. Click it.
  5. The sign-in screen loads. Paste your key and click Sign in.
  6. ChatGPT reloads within a few seconds, already connected.
  7. When it asks about the corpus, choose Always allow.

It tells you straight away which authors your key reaches. To start a conversation, write the author's name at the beginning of the sentence, or use the slash, as in Claude.

3 · When the subscription expires

Access runs for a year. You get an e-mail warning seven days before it expires, and after that there are three more days of grace in which everything keeps working.

Once the grace period passes without renewal, access pauses. Nothing is deleted: your authors stay reserved, and the key starts working again as soon as the renewal is paid — with no reinstalling and no pasting the key again.

On renewal the same key works again — no reinstalling the plugin, no pasting anything, no redoing the connection.

4 · If something doesn't work

The author answers like an ordinary assistant, without his voice. Call him with the slash — /steiner — in the first message. The slash is what loads the persona.

I installed it and it doesn't show up. In Settings → Connectors, check that compendia is connected and enabled, and that the permission is not blocked.

It works in one place and not the other. That is expected: the app, claude.ai, Claude Code and ChatGPT are separate installations. Repeat it in the ones you use.

It takes a while to answer. On the first question to each author, it loads the whole persona. The ones after that are faster.

The answer says the corpus doesn't cover my question. That is the system working, not failing. The Bibliotheca indexes the public-domain volumes of each author and nothing else — when the text does not cover the question, it says so instead of filling the gap with something plausible.

I lost my key. It is in the e-mail you received with the corpus. Reply to that e-mail and we will send it again — never generate a new one yourself.

The key is your licence. Don't share it: whoever has the code has your access.
