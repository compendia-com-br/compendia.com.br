Installation and use

Your key arrives by e-mail as soon as the payment clears. It starts with cmp_ and it is your licence — the key is what opens the corpus, wherever you use it.

Key not there yet? Pix and card clear immediately; bank slip and transfer take longer, and the e-mail only goes out after the payment is confirmed. If it still hasn't arrived after that, write to contato@compendia.com.br — we answer within 24 to 48 business hours.

1 · Install and activate it in Claude

This works in all three Claude surfaces: the desktop app, claude.ai in the browser, and Claude Code. The path is the same in all three. But installing in one does not install in the others: if you use more than one, repeat it there.

The screenshots and videos in this section show Claude in English. The Compendia sign-in screen is in Portuguese for now: the field is Chave de acesso (access key) and the button is Entrar (sign in).

Step 1 · Download the plugin

Download the plugin compendia-bibliotheca.zip

The file is small and holds no books. It only starts answering once you connect your key.

Step 2 · Install

  1. In Claude, click your name in the bottom-left corner.
  2. Go to Settings and, in the left menu, choose Plugins.
  3. In the top-right corner, click Add, then the option to upload a plugin.
  4. Drag compendia-bibliotheca.zip into the window, or click it to pick the file.
  5. Click the upload button.

Step 3 · Activate it with your key

  1. On the plugin page, open the Connectors tab. You will see compendia there. Click Connect.
  2. The Add custom connector window opens. Don't change anything in it: confirm with the button at the bottom right, and confirm again on the next screen.
  3. The Compendia sign-in screen opens. Paste your key, the one starting with cmp_, and click Entrar.
  4. Claude reloads and shows the notice Connected to compendia in the bottom-right corner.

Step 4 · Check the permission

In your name → Settings → Connectors, compendia should show as connected and enabled. Click it to see the permission: set it to Needs approval or Always allow. With the connector blocked, the corpus does not answer.

2 · Use it in ChatGPT

OpenAI has set 11 December 2026 as the deadline for custom GPTs to migrate to plugins. Until then, the Compendia Bibliotheca GPT works as usual. After that date, if you use it through ChatGPT you will need to move to the plugin. Your key and your authors stay the same: only the way in changes.

Which way in. In the ChatGPT desktop app, download the plugin just below. In the browser (chatgpt.com), use the custom GPT further down until the plugin is approved in OpenAI's directory. The plugin file does not install from the browser: that is OpenAI's rule, not Compendia's.

On the desktop: the plugin

Download the file and install it from the ChatGPT desktop app. Activation uses the same sign-in screen and the same cmp_ key you already have.

Download the ChatGPT plugin compendia-bibliotheca-chatgpt.zip

These screenshots and the video were recorded with ChatGPT in Portuguese. The steps below describe each button, so you can find it in English.

Install the plugin

  1. In the ChatGPT app, open the customise area in the left bar and choose Plugins.
  2. In the top-right corner, open the Add menu and choose the option to upload the plugin as a compressed file.
  1. In the new-plugin window, click the button to choose a file and pick compendia-bibliotheca-chatgpt.zip.
  2. Click the button to add the plugin, bottom right.
  1. The Compendia Bibliotheca page opens. Click the install button, top right.
  1. A notice says Compendia Bibliotheca is ready to use. Click the button to set up Compendia Bibliotheca.

Activate it with your key

  1. ChatGPT opens a setup conversation. It says the connection still needs checking: that is what the next steps do.
  2. Open ChatGPT Settings and go to the MCP servers page, in the MCPs tab.
  3. On the compendia line, click the authenticate button on the right. Don't touch the field next to it.
  1. The Compendia sign-in screen opens. Paste your key, the one starting with cmp_, and click Entrar.
  2. To use it, type @compendia in the conversation and pick Compendia Bibliotheca from the list.

The plugin has also been submitted to OpenAI's directory and is under review. Once it is approved, it will also work in the browser, and this guide will show how.

In the browser: the custom GPT (until 11 December 2026)

Nothing to download here. You only need the link and your key.

Open Compendia Bibliotheca in ChatGPT

  1. Sign in to ChatGPT and open the GPT with the button above. It is the same link as in the e-mail.
  2. Type a question, or click one of the suggestions.
  1. A button to sign in with corpus.compendia.com.br appears. Click it.
  2. On the sign-in screen, paste your key and click Entrar. ChatGPT comes back with a notice that your account is connected.
  3. When it asks permission to talk to the corpus, choose Always allow.

3 · Ways to use it

Talk to an author. Open a new conversation and start with a slash and the author's name, for instance /steiner, followed by your question. In ChatGPT, writing the author's name at the start of the sentence also works.

See who your key reaches. Ask to list authors.

Ask for the citation. Ask for the exact sentence: «quote the passage where Kant talks about…». The answer gives the volume and the sentence, and the page when the source volume preserves it.

Look for a theme in the corpus. Ask where a subject appears: «in which works does Rousseau deal with the education of the senses?». The search runs through the volumes your key covers.

Put two authors face to face. After the first one answers, call the second and ask what they make of it. Each answer comes from reading one corpus, with the citation attached.

One author at a time, on purpose. The Bibliotheca loads each author's way of answering separately, so two voices never get mixed into one answer. That is why switching authors takes a few extra seconds.

In Claude, creating a Project is optional. It keeps a separate conversation with each author.

4 · When the subscription expires

Access runs for a year. You get an e-mail warning seven days before it expires, and after that there are three more days of grace in which everything keeps working.

Once the grace period passes without renewal, access pauses. Nothing is deleted: your authors stay reserved, and the key starts working again as soon as the renewal is paid — with no reinstalling and no pasting the key again.

On renewal the same key works again — no reinstalling the plugin, no pasting anything, no redoing the connection.

5 · If something doesn't work

The author answers like an ordinary assistant, without their voice. Call them with the slash — /steiner — in the first message. The slash is what loads the persona.

I installed it and it doesn't show up. In Settings → Connectors, check that compendia is connected and enabled, and that the permission is not blocked.

It works in one place and not the other. That is expected: the app, claude.ai, Claude Code and ChatGPT are separate installations, and in ChatGPT so are the GPT and the plugin. Repeat it in the ones you use.

It takes a while to answer. On the first question to each author, it loads the whole persona. The ones after that are faster.

The answer says the corpus doesn't cover my question. That is the system working, not failing. The Bibliotheca indexes the public-domain volumes of each author and nothing else — when the text does not cover the question, it says so instead of filling the gap with something plausible.

I lost my key. It is in the e-mail you received with the corpus. Reply to that e-mail and we will send it again — never generate a new one yourself.

The key is your licence. Don't share it: whoever has the code has your access.
